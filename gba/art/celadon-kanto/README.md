# Celadon City ten years on (Kanto, GBA)

Status: planning. The change plot is waiting on the owner's approval; nothing is built yet.

Method: [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md), "Kanto towns" and "Planning a new town". FireRed's
Celadon (`CeladonCity_Frlg`, 60x40, General_Frlg + CeladonCity) is the base. HGSS's Celadon
(pokeheartgold header 55, area bank 15, zone T07) is the plan reference.

## Sources

- `source/hgss/`: HGSS's Celadon rendered from its own map data
  (`python3 tools/gba/maps/celadon/hgss_source.py PATH/TO/pokeheartgold`)
- `source/hgss-events.json`: T07's warps, signs (English text) and objects, in map cells
- `evidence/plan.png`: the change plot (HGSS lettered, FireRed numbered)

## What HGSS changed against FireRed

- A. The fenced pond became a north-south canal with a footbridge on the street.
- B. The fountain plaza opened into a larger lawn with an organic edge.
- C, D. The Dept Store and Condominiums stand side by side on the north row.
- E. Game Corner and prize corner stay at the centre.
- F. The gym stands on a paved forecourt between two houses.
- G. The restaurant has its own awning front in the south-east row.
- No hotel.

## Change list (draft)

1. Market: stalls (Slateport market art) around a wider fountain plaza (B).
2. Services building: the Dept Store's FireRed shell with a market awning; salon, tutor and
   EV training on the sign.
3. Botanical Gardens on the gym site: a glasshouse (Lilycove Contest Hall roof, reglazed),
   beds and hedges for the ledges, a paved forecourt (F).
4. Hotel: the south row's last two houses merged into one two-story building, into the
   Condominiums interior cut to 1F and 2F; the Mansion door takes FireRed's Hotel interior.
5. Café between the market and the gardens, awning front (G), into FireRed's Restaurant;
   the Restaurant's old door becomes a house.
6. Game Corner and prize room kept (E).
7. Bike lane from Route 7 along the main road and west avenue to Route 16.
8. Canal from the pond south to the treeline, a footbridge on the south street (A).

Also: street trees where the route check passes, the new city sign, the Rocket grunts
removed. Out of this pass: dialogue, the Route 16 gate guard, interior furnishing.
